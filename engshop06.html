Story #01

Introduction to my site

Published on October 5, 2026 • By Diana

My name is Diana and I am of Asian origin, specifically from the Philippines, and I currently live in Portugal since my divorce, after 20 years spent in France and being a mother of three children. Sparkling and with a bit of mischief, I have always loved grabbing life by the horns without ever taking myself too seriously.

Today, as I cross the threshold of 40, I felt the need to share my experiences. Reaching this milestone is a special moment: you know yourself better, you embrace yourself more fully, and above all, you have much less time to waste on the unnecessary!

I wanted a place that is mine, a cozy little cocoon without filters where I can reveal a bit more of myself, tell you my daily anecdotes, my adventures and share my universe with you.

Some of you might know me from the many other sites where our paths have crossed. My sexuality didn't begin in adulthood, and those sites aren't too fond of stories from my younger years—or certain other practices I enjoy. I simply wanted a place of my own—a small, warm, and unfiltered site where I can open up a bit more, share my daily anecdotes and adventures, and let you into my world.

Make yourself comfortable, treat yourself, and welcome to my world. This is just the beginning...
